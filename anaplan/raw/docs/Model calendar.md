English

The Model calendar endpoints are used to retrieve and update the model’s current planning period and fiscal-year settings.

These endpoints provide programmatic control over the model’s time context. They help determine what period and fiscal year is this model currently using, and whether you can update those settings as part of an automated planning cycle.
