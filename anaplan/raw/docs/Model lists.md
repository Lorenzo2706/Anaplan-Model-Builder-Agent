English

Model lists endpoints are used to discover, inspect, retrieve, and maintain list structures and list items within an Anaplan model.

They provide list discovery, list-item retrieval, and transactional list maintenance and help find which lists exist in this model, what items they contain, and how can you add, update, delete, or maintain list members without running a file-based import.
