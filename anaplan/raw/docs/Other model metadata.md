English

Other model metadata endpoints are used to retrieve information about dimensions and their items, especially when an integration needs to understand or resolve the structure of a model before working with data.

These endpoints provide dimension-item discovery and lookup. They help answer questions like: “What members exist in this dimension, how do I identify a specific item, and which valid item IDs or codes should I use when reading, writing, mapping, or validating model data?”
