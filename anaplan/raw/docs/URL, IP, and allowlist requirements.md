This page contains the Uniform Resource Locators (URLs), Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, and domains that are associated with the Anaplan platform.

To receive notifications about updates to this page, please [subscribe here](https://community.anaplan.com/discussion/154802/how-to-subscribe-to-domain-and-ip-range-updates?sort=-dateInserted) .

If your company restricts traffic, use this information to update your allowlist and ensure continued access to the Anaplan platform. For example, if your company restricts outbound connectivity through the use of web filtering technologies, then you need to allowlist all the entries in the “Outbound traffic domains and IPs” section for the Anaplan region(s) you use. If you don't do this, Anaplan won't function as expected.

Similarly, if your company restricts inbound connectivity, for example, through the use of firewalls or access control lists (ACLs), then you need to allowlist all the entries in the “Inbound traffic IPs” section for the Anaplan region(s) you use. If you don't do this, then data integrations such as CloudWorks™ or Anaplan Data Orchestrator won't function as expected.

Cloudflare and Cloudfront IPs can change without prior warning. Please ensure you have an appropriate process in place to account for this.
