[Workflow](https://help.anaplan.com/workflow-d3ef84e7-e883-4b38-af79-889ea825df9f "Workflow")

Enterprise business processes are often complex, non-linear, or require flexibility based on the data they are designed to manage. Workflow Advanced adds extra functionality and flexibility to Anaplan’s standard set of Workflow features to further meet the needs of the complex organizations that Anaplan supports.

Workflow Advanced enables you to:

- Create parallel steps in a workflow.
- Branch (and reconnect branches) to build complex workflows with data-driven branching logic.
- Drive automation by configuring a value-based decision task to query the data in a model and make a decision based on the model value.
- Create send-back loops to add flexibility, review and rework cycles to decision tasks.

**Note:** Workflow Advanced is an entitled service and requires a separate subscription to enable the feature. Your Account Executive can assist you with this. The features are tenant-specific, so in a multi-tenant environment, the Workflow Advanced features only display on tenants that have the feature enabled.
